Frequently Asked Questions

Is GoDaddy India better than BigRock for India?

For most Indian users, GoDaddy India edges ahead with a 4.2/5 rating. It offers most recognised hosting brand worldwide and huge domain name selection at competitive prices. However, BigRock may suit you better if indian businesses is your primary need.

Which is cheaper — GoDaddy India or BigRock?

BigRock is cheaper at ₹59/month vs ₹99/month. Remember to check renewal prices too — GoDaddy India renews at ₹499/mo and BigRock at ₹299/mo.

Do GoDaddy India and BigRock have India data centres?

BigRock has an India data centre while the other does not India-based servers. An India data centre reduces page load time by 40–80ms for Indian visitors, which matters for SEO.

Which has better customer support — GoDaddy India or BigRock?

Both offer 24/7 support, but GoDaddy India scores 5/5 and BigRock scores 3/5 on support in our tests. GoDaddy provides Hindi phone support which is valuable for Indian customers.

Can I migrate my website from BigRock to GoDaddy India?

Yes. GoDaddy India makes migration straightforward using its control panel migration tool. Most migrations complete within 2–4 hours with zero downtime.
