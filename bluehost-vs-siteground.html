Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bluehost India better than SiteGround for India?

For most Indian users, SiteGround edges ahead with a 4.7/5 rating. It offers top-tier speed powered by google cloud infrastructure and free cdn + cloudflare enterprise included. However, Bluehost India may suit you better if wordpress sites is your primary need.

Which is cheaper — Bluehost India or SiteGround?

Bluehost India is cheaper at ₹199/month vs ₹499/month. Remember to check renewal prices too — Bluehost India renews at ₹599/mo and SiteGround at ₹1499/mo.

Do Bluehost India and SiteGround have India data centres?

SiteGround has an India data centre while the other does not India-based servers. An India data centre reduces page load time by 40–80ms for Indian visitors, which matters for SEO.

Which has better customer support — Bluehost India or SiteGround?

Both offer 24/7 support, but Bluehost India scores 5/5 and SiteGround scores 5/5 on support in our tests. SiteGround provides Hindi phone support which is valuable for Indian customers.

Can I migrate my website from Bluehost India to SiteGround?

Yes. SiteGround offers free site migration using its Site Tools migration tool. Most migrations complete within 2–4 hours with zero downtime.
